About the course

Generative AI for Work & Research is an 8 week, live online certification focused on responsible, practical AI for productivity—covering content creation, automated data analysis, research & text processing, custom chatbots, and a hands on capstone project.

Key themes

  • Documents, presentations & websites
  • Automated data analysis
  • GenAI for research & text analysis
  • Custom GPTs & chatbots

Format

Live workshops and help sessions in an online classroom, with practical assignments and peer learning.
